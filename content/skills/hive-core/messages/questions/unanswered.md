# A card returns unanswered

Read from [questions](../questions.md) when a card comes back without answers.

Follow the host's required response first. The answers the card did return stand.

- **A card that carried the round alone closed by itself:** an empty answer or a timeout with no rejection, as a Codex card after about 120 seconds. Write in one message every open decision and finding of the round in full, those not yet asked included, ending with a waiting line saying "ok" applies every starred option and a reply such as `D2 B` changes one.
- **A card after a round written in the text** comes back empty: add one line that the card closed and names the decisions still open.
- **The user left it:** a rejection, dismissal or decline, also as an error or after the host's own timeout; the host ended the turn with the card open; a request to talk; answers to only some questions; or free text that adds, removes or replaces a decision or an option. Never write the round out on your own; act on what they said:
  - Answers by ID or a plain acceptance: apply them and ask the rest through the card again; if the cancel was a mistake or they ask for the questions, ask the same round again, same IDs.
  - The direction or premise changed, or a decision or option was added, removed or replaced: rework those decisions and ask them as a new round with new IDs.
  - They drop the questions: drop them, except one a later step cannot do without, asked again with that reason.
  - Another subject or question: answer it and end with one line naming the decisions still open and how to get them back; ask them through the card when the user says so or asks to continue work that needs them.

  With nothing said about the decisions, reply with that one line alone. A decision asked again keeps its ID, after one line naming which return. Later rounds still go through the card.
