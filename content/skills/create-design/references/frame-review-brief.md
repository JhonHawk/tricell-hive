# Frame review brief

Read when briefing the `hive-review-ux` child that reviews frames drawn in a design file, at the review of [example record](example-record.md), its one follow-up review in [rule cards](rule-cards.md), or the review of the [mobile phase](approval.md#mobile-phase). The rest of [reviewer brief](../../hive-core/delegation/reviewer-brief.md) still applies.

Frames have no states to reach, so the brief gives:

- the file, and the node and image of each frame drawn;
- the nodes of the sibling screens it follows, with what they fix, as step 3 of [create-design](../SKILL.md) lists it;
- the decisions recorded for the app, with the corrections read from the siblings' example records;
- each frame's width, as [draw](draw.md#width) sets;
- the design library the stage bound, declared or not, or that it bound none, with the record's inventory and `candidate:` lines and whether the file could use it;
- the request and research's decisions, as requirements.

It leaves out the drawer's list of proposed business rules, so the reviewer finds the rules nothing settles on its own. Only the brief of the one follow-up review also gives each rule card's node with its frame's and the record's `rule:` lines of those screens, as [rule cards](rule-cards.md) sets.

In the mobile phase, the brief also gives the approved web frame of each mobile frame, which the mobile frame must follow (under unattended delegation, the reviewed web frame, named as not approved), and the node of the twin section; as above, only its one follow-up review gets the cards, with their «on mobile» rows.
