# Design in Figma: draw, correct, review, record

Pick the entry by what the request asks.

| Entry | When | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draw | A new or recomposed screen before the plan, or a design as the deliverable | Steps 1 to 8 |
| Correct | A request that names existing frames as its target | Steps 1 to 8 with [special cases](references/special-cases.md#a-request-for-the-design-itself) |
| Review an existing design | A Figma link or an image of a design someone else drew | [review](references/review.md); draws nothing |
| Create or refresh `DESIGN.md` | The user asks, or a UI plan needs it | [UI criteria](references/ui-criteria.md) and [design record](references/design-record.md#writing) |

## Design a screen in Figma before the plan

An example is a drawing of one screen, a frame in the project's Figma file, that the user picks or corrects before the work is planned. It settles what the screen is; the plan, the build and the verification then compare against it. This stage is optional and publishes nothing to the viewer. It writes only in that Figma file, in the work's folder under `_support/workspace/` and, at step 7, the rules of the work's ticket; it edits nothing in the repository.

Enter by the user's typed command, by their choice of this stage in the next-step block of [hive-mode](../hive-mode/SKILL.md), or by continuation from research for a request whose deliverable is the design. That reply or request is the user's instruction for the writes this stage makes to the Figma file step 3 resolves, limited to what the work creates. It replaces no question a critical effect takes: a write that spends money or paid quota still gets its own.

Follow the steps in order.

1. **Check the connection** as [design stage](../hive-core/ui/design-stage.md) sets. Without it, draw nothing and report the blocker with what is missing: for a screen that will be built, end with the next-step block toward [flow-plan](../flow-plan/SKILL.md), which draws its text sketches; for a request whose deliverable is the design, the only route is connecting Figma.
2. **Load before drawing.** Read the usage instructions the Figma connection ships, when it ships any, such as a skill it marks as required before writing to the canvas. Then load the project's design system as [UI criteria](references/ui-criteria.md) sets: the app's `DESIGN.md`, its library and its customization layer; and the decisions already recorded for the app in the **Decisions** of its `DESIGN.md`. Also gather the sources that settle a business rule, as step 5 uses them: the request, the ticket with its criteria and comments, research's decisions as the conversation, the ticket or the work's record holds them, and those recorded decisions. Draw nothing before all of them are read.
3. **Resolve the file** as the point «Resolving the file in the stage» of [design stage](../hive-core/ui/design-stage.md) sets. Then read the file: when the connection cannot write to it, such as with view-only access, draw nothing and report it as step 1 sets. Read the screen's siblings, the frames of the same module or navigation in that file and the screens of its code, and measure them: they fix the app shell, header, toolbar, table, row actions, whether a form is a page or a dialog, and spacing. Then read and follow [design library](references/design-library.md): the siblings' records, the library to bind, the inventory, the pieces to reuse, and how steps 6 and 7 record the file and library.
4. **Say what the user will do by hand.** Before the first drawing, list each step in Figma only the user can take, such as publishing or adding the bound library, and when it comes; or say there is none.
5. **Draw and record.** When this work's record already lists screens stopped by a `scope` rule, as after research settled it, write each such rule research settled as `approved <date>: <answer>` and draw only those screens; the others keep their status. Otherwise, draw the examples per screen that [design stage](../hive-core/ui/design-stage.md) sets, following its siblings and the recorded decisions. Read [draw](references/draw.md) before drawing: each frame's width, what you may change in the file, and how the main thread draws. Draw with the project's tokens and components, the real labels, and data of realistic volume, on a page named after the work, grouped as [sections](references/sections.md) sets: read it before drawing. When the work has more than one screen, first draw, at its main width only, the one that uses the most inventory pieces, record it, have it alone reviewed and that review reconciled as this step sets for the set, without cards or a follow-up, and give the user its location without asking or ending the turn; then draw the others, and the 440 px frame of each web screen served on mobile, from its pieces. The review below covers the whole set, that screen too.

   As soon as the frames are drawn, before the review and before asking anything, read [example record](references/example-record.md) and follow it: list the business rules, as [business rules](../hive-core/business/business-rules.md) defines them, that the frames show and step 2's sources leave unsettled and record each as `proposed`, `assumption` or `scope` (a `scope` rule stops the screens it shapes); write the record in `examples.md`; have one `hive-review-ux` child review the frames; fix the findings that hold; draw the rule cards beside the frames as [rule cards](references/rule-cards.md) sets; and have only the corrected frames and the cards reviewed again once.
6. **Choose.** Read [approval](references/approval.md). Finish drawing and reviewing the work's screens, including every frame [draw](references/draw.md) sets, before asking approval, and list screens stopped by a `scope` rule apart. Ask one approval for the completed set, never per screen, and in the same round each `proposed` rule as a decision of its own, once per convention whether to record it in `DESIGN.md`, and whether to record the file or the library.
7. **Record the approval** in the turn the user answers, as [approval](references/approval.md#record-the-approval) sets: each rule's and approved screen's line, the answers about conventions, the file and the library, and the work's rules in its ticket, as [business rules ticket](../hive-core/business/business-ticket.md) sets; the alternatives not chosen are removed.
8. **Close the stage** as [approval](references/approval.md#close-the-stage) sets: with a `scope` rule in the record, end toward [hive-mode](../hive-mode/SKILL.md) naming it; otherwise, once every screen is approved, end toward `flow-plan`. A request whose deliverable is the design is the instruction for these steps, and its target, not-done state and close follow [special cases](references/special-cases.md#a-request-for-the-design-itself).

## Unattended delegation

While [unattended delegation](../unattended-delegation/SKILL.md) is active, ask none of the questions of steps 3 and 6, and follow [special cases](references/special-cases.md#unattended-delegation).
