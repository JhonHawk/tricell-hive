# Support material

Read at [flow-close](../SKILL.md) step 2, at every close that takes the inventory of a work's support material, before its final report lists that material, and before the first push of a work's branch.

List what this work created or changed under the project's support home, in its checkout or worktree: its folders under `sessions/`, `workspace/` and `evidence/`, found by the work's identifier as [layout](../../project-layout/references/layout.md) names it, its change folder when the work was abandoned, and any other support path its checkpoint or its commits name. Step 5 removes its reproducible temporaries from the host's temporary location. Name material of other work or uncertain ownership, such as an untracked folder no record ties to this work, as kept, without an action. Propose one action for each item, with its reason:

- **Commit:** a curated, shareable record under `sessions/` that is not yet in Git; never verification material, a secret, a raw dump or reproducible output.
- **Archive:** a `sessions/` folder whose records all read closed and that holds nothing **Commit** excludes, moved as [archive](../../project-layout/references/archive.md) sets once the answer selects it; the commit below delivers the move.
- **Promote:** a conclusion [Retain evidence](../../project-layout/references/layout.md#retain-evidence) sends to a durable document under `docs/`, as [inventory](../../project-layout/references/inventory.md#promote-a-conclusion) sets.
- **Delete:** an item under `workspace/`, or an uncommitted one under `evidence/`, with positive evidence it is reproducible or a kept copy replaces it, such as verification material a merged pull request's body replaces, which includes a capture only when its uploaded URL is in that body, read again at the close; never a finding's trace without a ticket, unique evidence of unintegrated work, a dump or export, or uncommitted work.
- **Keep:** anything else, with where it stays; an item in a worktree stays at the same path in the main checkout.

## Before the push

When the delivery commits, take the inventory before the first push of the work's branch and settle there the commit, archive and promote items: under `interactive`, ask them in verify's validation round, in the format below; under `automatic` or `ready`, apply each proposed action and report it with how to revert it; under unattended delegation, its step 5 answers. Commit them on the work's branch before the push. Deletions and kept items wait for the close.

## At the close

When items not settled before the push, such as deletions, have an action other than keep, ask them as one decision in its own round, as [decision format](../../hive-rule-questions/SKILL.md) sets, opening with each item to delete, its size and reason, and their total, then the other actions, and the kept items in one line: the starred option applies every action, another keeps everything, and a reply naming an item's path changes its action. When the repository holds versioned legacy material no work of this session created, such as an `evidence/` or `archive/` folder, add one line with its count, its size and the [project-layout](../../project-layout/SKILL.md) command, in that decision or else in the report, and never act on it here. Nothing is moved, committed or deleted before the answer, which unattended delegation gives at its step 5; apply it before steps 4–5 continue. Commit the committed, archived and promoted items with step 4's move or, when no change folder is archived, in one commit that touches only the support home; either goes on a new branch from the base as [git-workflow](../../git-workflow/SKILL.md) sets, never on the base unless the delivery is `direct-base`, and is delivered as the work's delivery mode sets, before step 5 cleans up its branch with the work's. Work with no delivery mode, such as an operational task, proposes no commit, archive or promotion. Work that is paused, held under `hold`, or waiting at an open pull request lists its items as pending, asks nothing and acts on none, in every close, an unattended one included; the full or follow-up close asks the decision.
