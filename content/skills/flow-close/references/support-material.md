# Support material

Read at [flow-close](../SKILL.md) step 2, and before the final report lists support material.

List what this work created or changed under the project's support home, in its checkout or worktree: its work folder under `workspace/`, plan file included, and any item of a legacy top-level `evidence/`, found by the work's identifier as [layout](../../project-layout/references/layout.md) names it, and any other support path its checkpoint or its commits name. Step 5 removes its reproducible temporaries from the host's temporary location. Name material of other work or uncertain ownership, such as an untracked folder no record ties to this work, as kept, without an action. Assign one action to each item, with its reason:

- **Delete:** an item under `workspace/`, or an uncommitted one under `evidence/`, with positive evidence it is reproducible or a kept copy replaces it: verification material whose attachment the ticket's attachment list shows, as step 1 read it back, or that the merged pull request's body replaces, including a capture that [Retain evidence](../../project-layout/references/layout.md#retain-evidence) counts as replaced; never a finding's trace without a ticket, unique evidence of unintegrated work, a dump or export, or uncommitted work.
- **Promote:** a conclusion [Retain evidence](../../project-layout/references/layout.md#retain-evidence) sends to a durable document under `docs/`, as [inventory](../../project-layout/references/inventory.md#promote-a-conclusion) sets.
- **Keep:** anything else, with where it stays; the plan file always stays, as local material the retention of [project-layout](../../project-layout/SKILL.md) prunes; an item in a worktree stays at the same path in the main checkout.

The step asks nothing. Apply each deletion; move what is kept from the worktree to the same paths in the main checkout before step 5 removes the worktree, since removing a worktree deletes its ignored files. Support material stays unversioned: this close never commits or archives it. Name in the report's **Next** each promotion, with the [project-layout](../../project-layout/SKILL.md) command that applies it. When the repository holds versioned legacy material no work of this session created, such as a `sessions/`, `evidence/` or `archive/` folder, add one line with its count, its size and that command, and never act on it here. Work with no delivery mode, such as an operational task, proposes no promotion. Work that is paused, held under `hold`, or waiting at an open pull request lists its items as pending and acts on none, in every close, an unattended one included.
